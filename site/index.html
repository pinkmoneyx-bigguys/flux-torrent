Flux Torrent

A torrent client for Windows that starts playing a film while it is still downloading. Tracker search with posters, a media library and RSS, on the qBittorrent engine.

Download for Windows Portable version

Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit. Free and open source.

Search the trackers, see the films

Results come grouped into film cards with posters. A card shows every release in full, with its quality, HDR, source and voice-overs, the plot from Wikipedia in your language and a trailer. Only films whose titles have your words stay.

Everything you downloaded, on one shelf

The media library finds every video in your torrents: one poster per film or series. A film plays from its poster; a series opens its episodes by season, with what you have watched and what is still on its way.

New episodes come by themselves

Follow a tracker's or a show's RSS feed and read it in Flux. Auto-download rules pick up a new episode in the quality you want as soon as it is out.

Watch while it downloads
The built-in player asks for the pieces right ahead of the playhead first. Watch without downloading keeps the film in a cache and clears it after.
The qBittorrent engine
qBittorrent 5.2 and libtorrent 2.0 underneath: the same downloads, limits, queues and trackers, in a new interface.
Moves from qBittorrent
On the first start Flux brings your torrents and settings over in one click.
Nine languages
English, Русский, Українська, Deutsch, Español, Français, Português, Polski and 简体中文, switched on the fly.
Updates itself
A new version is a click away, checked against its SHA-256 before it installs.
Keeps going in the tray
Close the window and Flux keeps downloading and seeding, with the speeds by the clock.

Get Flux

Questions

Windows calls the installer an “unrecognized app”. Is it safe?

SmartScreen warns about every new program without a paid code-signing certificate, and Flux has none yet. Click “More info”, then “Run anyway”. To make sure the file is the one published, compare its SHA-256 with SHA256SUMS.txt from the release.

How is it different from qBittorrent?

The engine is the same, the interface is new: a player that plays while downloading, search with posters and film cards, a media library, and a look of its own. Your qBittorrent settings and torrents move over on the first start.

How do I remove Flux?

Open Settings, Apps, Installed apps, find Flux Torrent and choose Uninstall. It asks whether to keep your settings and torrent list, and never touches the files you downloaded. The portable version is just a folder: delete it.

Something doesn't work. Where do I tell you?

Open an issue on GitHub: the form asks for the Flux version and the steps. English or Russian are both fine.
